# Cohort A Learner Handbook

## Cohort identifier
The official identifier is `cohort-a`.

## Communication expectations
Learners should communicate professionally and provide enough context when requesting support.

## Submission policy
Cohort A submissions are accepted through the learner portal.

## Attendance policy
Learners should notify Operations before missing a scheduled session whenever possible.

## Cohort isolation
Cohort A learners must receive answers only from Cohort A approved materials.
