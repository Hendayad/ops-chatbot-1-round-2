All sessions will take place online through the program meeting link.

The weekly task must be submitted before Friday at 11:59 PM Cairo time.

Late submissions require approval from the Operations team. 

## **8. Who should I contact for technical questions?** 

Send technical questions to your assigned mentor. 

## **9. Who should I contact for administrative issues?** 

Post administrative or account-access issues in the official internship group and mention the administrators. 

## **10. What happens when the chatbot cannot answer?** 

The chatbot should say that the answer is not available in the approved materials and request human support.
