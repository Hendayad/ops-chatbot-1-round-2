# Cohort A Getting Started Guide

## Step 1: Sign in
Use the email address registered for Cohort A to sign in to the Operations Support Portal.

## Step 2: Complete your profile
Add your preferred name and timezone when requested.

## Step 3: Join the cohort workspace
Use the Cohort A workspace invitation provided by Operations.

## Step 4: Review the schedule
Read the Cohort A schedule before attending the first session.

## Step 5: Ask operational questions
The chatbot may answer only from approved Cohort A materials. If the answer is unavailable, the issue should be escalated to Operations.
