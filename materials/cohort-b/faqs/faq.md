# Cohort B Frequently Asked Questions

## Attendance
Learners in Cohort B should attend every scheduled session. If a learner cannot attend, they should notify the Operations team before the session begins.

## Task submission
Cohort B tasks must be submitted through the learner portal. Submissions sent through email or chat are not considered official unless Operations confirms otherwise.

## Final project deadline
The Cohort B final project deadline is **August 27, 2026 at 11:59 PM Cairo time**.

## Support
For operational questions not answered in approved materials, learners should request help from the Operations team.
